## 💰 How to Use Invoicing

### Create an Invoice
1. Go to **Admin → Invoices**
2. Click **Create Invoice** button
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Client Name**: Who is this invoice for?
   - **Client Email**: Client's email address
   - **Project Name**: What project is this for?
   - **Due Date**: When should it be paid?

4. Add line items:
   - **Description**: What service/product?
   - **Quantity**: How many?
   - **Rate**: Price per unit
   - Click **+ Add Item** for more items

5. Set tax percentage (if applicable)
6. Add notes (payment terms, etc.)
7. Click **Create Invoice**

### Manage Invoices
- **View**: Click the eye icon 👁️ to see details
- **Mark as Paid**: Click the checkmark ✓ when payment received
- **Edit**: Click the pencil icon ✏️ to modify
- **Delete**: Click the trash icon 🗑️ to remove

### Invoice Statuses
- 📝 **Draft** - Created but not sent
- 📤 **Sent** - Sent to client
- ✅ **Paid** - Payment received
- ⚠️ **Overdue** - Past due date
- ❌ **Cancelled** - Invoice cancelled

### Features
- ✅ Auto-calculate totals
- ✅ Invoice numbering
- ✅ Status tracking
- ✅ Search and filter
- ✅ Revenue statistics

### Tips
- Invoice numbers are auto-generated
- Totals calculate automatically
- Use status to track payments
- Filter by status to find specific invoices

## 🔧 Data Storage

All data is stored in your browser's localStorage:
- ✅ Messages persist after refresh
- ✅ Invoices are saved automatically
- ✅ Notifications stay until cleared
- ✅ Analytics data is always current

### Backup Your Data
To backup your data:
1. Open browser developer tools (F12)
2. Go to Application/Storage tab
3. Find localStorage
4. Export the following keys:
   - `freelancer_messages`
   - `freelancer_invoices`
   - `freelancer_notifications`
